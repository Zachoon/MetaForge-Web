## Evidence this stream uses

Expert-authored **reasoning** (the student's work), not decklists alone:

- Tournament reports with strategic explanation
- Pro / Hall of Fame articles and interviews
- Feature-match commentary (when available)
- Coaching writeups that explain *why*

Decklists remain Evidence Stream 001 territory (structure / preparation).  
This stream asks whether **why** concepts recur independently.

## Smallest honest pipeline (when implementation is authorized)

1. Select a **tiny** corpus of independent expert sources (start with a handful, not the internet).  
2. Extract candidate decision concepts in player language (tempo, inevitability, redundancy, threat assessment, sequencing, role assignment, risk, opportunity cost — only if the text earns them).  
3. Require **replication across ≥2 independent experts** before a candidate is recorded.  
4. Record rejects and contradictions in Archive.  
5. Atlas may later admit vocabulary — only under Atlas rules.  
6. Laboratory may open **only** if a candidate earns a single testable hypothesis.

Stop after one paper / one observation report. Do not expand the corpus for completeness.
