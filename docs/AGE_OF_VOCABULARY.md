# The Age of Vocabulary

**Era:** active **discipline layer** under [Strategic Cognition](STRATEGIC_COGNITION.md)  
**Date named:** 2026-08-11  
**Inflection:** from “make the engine smarter” → “what *is* intelligence in Commander?” → **“how do we observe strategic thought?”**

Vocabulary is necessary. It is not the mission.

The mission is Strategic Cognition: reverse-engineer how elite players think. Atlas admits words only when observation earns them — including future **Decision Patterns**, not only Capabilities.

---

## The shift

For months the project asked:

> How do we make the engine smarter?

The question is now:

> What *is* intelligence in Commander?

Those are different problems. The second is harder. It is also the one that can make MetaForge genuinely different from every other Commander tool.

---

## Ascent (not feature creep)

Research has been drifting upward in abstraction — forced by evidence, not by fashion:

```
Cards
  ↓
Interactions
  ↓
Topology
  ↓
Plans
  ↓
Capabilities
  ↓
Coverage
  ↓
Reasoning / Understanding (Mentor)
```

Falsified along the way: more interaction as primitive · topology-over-count · coefficient-only Brain v2.

---

## This era’s priority

**Make Atlas astonishingly good.**

Not because MetaForge needs more code.  
Because the hardest AI systems are usually limited not by algorithms, but by the **language available to express what they’ve learned**.

If Atlas becomes rich enough — capabilities, seats, multidimensional coverage, equivalence — Brain almost writes itself later. Chasing Brain v2 embodiment now would reverse the Constitution.

Illustrative Atlas richness (target, not shipped):

```
Capability: Commander Protection
Seats:
  Persistent · Stack · Sacrifice · Recursion
Implementations:
  Greaves · Flawless Maneuver · Dauntless Escort
  Malakir Rebirth · Brought Back
```

That is already closer to expert reasoning than `Greaves: score = 84.7`.

## Discipline

> Naming is not promotion.  
> **Elegance is not evidence.**

Some of Coverage / Seat / Capability will survive live Academy runs. Some will not. The system is allowed to discover which is which — that is the point.

Do not invent beautiful abstractions faster than Atlas can stabilize them or Academy can control them.

## Success of the era (not of Brain v2)

The Age of Vocabulary succeeds when:

1. Atlas terms stay stable across papers (or revisions are logged)  
2. Equivalence lets decks be compared by seats without shared cards  
3. Coverage stays multidimensional  
4. Mentor (when built) can draft coach-quality explanations **without** changing the 99  
5. Brain v1 remains healthy for users throughout  

Brain v2 embodiment is a **later** era — after vocabulary and understanding are earned.
