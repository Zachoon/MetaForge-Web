## What this means for users

Current users get Brain v1 + Honest Coach, plus (as of 2026-08-16) one small first look at Atlas/Mentor language in the card inspector — explicitly labeled experimental, zero construction effect.

In parallel, Academy and the Knowledge Expansion Program observe elite reality across six shipped epics. Construction does not inherit until the ladder clears — Epic 6's own shadow findings already name specific candidate gaps (interaction density, tempo, sequencing, redundancy) but none has cleared a Validation Harness report yet.

Forbidden: "make the Brain smarter" as the primary agent mission, inventing A4 from emptiness, inventing institutions when an evidence stream would do, or promoting concepts because they sound expert.
